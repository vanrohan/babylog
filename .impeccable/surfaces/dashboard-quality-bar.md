# Dashboard acceptance criteria

- Keep the existing Babylog palette and controls.
- Show Feeding, Sleep, Nappy, Bath, and Other activities on the timeline.
- Show daily pee and poo counts without requiring a detail selection.
- Provide exact timestamps, durations, findings, and notes as text.
- Distinguish active timers, unknown durations, and empty dates.
- Preserve selected ranges, chart state, and unsaved entry drafts during navigation and polling.
- Keep horizontal chart scrolling inside the viewport on phones.
- Support touch, mouse, and keyboard inspection.

Show recorded care only. Do not infer missing end times or provide medical advice.
